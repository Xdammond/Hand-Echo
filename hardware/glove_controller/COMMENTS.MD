In file R433A, our original understanding when we first purchased the component was 2 port SAW resonator. As we were testing, we began to see destabilization and oscillation dying. Without a wiring diagram for the part, we asked our professors and they all agreed that it was a two port. We then recreated a two-port system of the Colpitts oscillator but effectively it did not behave like a normal Colpitts Oscillator. It was only till March 15th, 2026, did we discover the true schematic of the component we had and then reworked the circuit back to a one-port design to have stability and ringing. 

The 2N5770 was not the intended BJT nor component we truly wanted to use. But due to our budget, we stuck with the RF BJT because it helped stabilize the circuit and allowed for the proper gain.
