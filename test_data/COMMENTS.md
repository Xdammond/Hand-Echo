Our first test of the circuit came from analyzing an oscilloscope signal we had obtained. At first, we wondered why there was no oscillation and figured our circuit design was bad. As we moved through the weeks, it came to our attention that oscilloscope we were working with did not read up 400MHz and was effectively capped at 150MHz for a reading. We also discovered that using a bread board at 200MHz and above causes distortion inside the signal making what we were testing incorrect and data invaluable.
